# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, including with newcomers and with people who write in a second language;
- give and accept feedback on the work, not the person;
- assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language or imagery;
- publishing someone's private information (an address, a phone number, a real name behind a handle) without
  their explicit permission. In a project about scams and threat actors this includes posting victims' or
  suspects' personal data in issues: redact it;
- using the project to attack, dox or harass anyone, or to share stolen data;
- sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when you
represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, **fj4rr**, privately (the contact on the maintainer's GitHub
profile). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues,
and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, in proportion to what
happened.
