    JATMP documentation¶

    JATMP is a self-hosted platform for spotting cyber threats and online fraud as they emerge. It reads security news, social media, threat-intelligence platforms and scam and fraud sources, and keeps track of:

    • Cyber threats: malware and ransomware families and their campaigns, threat actors, vulnerabilities (with CISA KEV, EPSS and CVSS context), ATT&CK techniques, and indicators of compromise checked against intel providers. → Analysis
    • Scams and fraud: scam types such as investment and loan fraud, phishing, malicious APKs and crypto drainers, as well as impersonated banks, e-wallets and government bodies, scammer identifiers and reported losses. → Scams and fraud
    • Critical infrastructure: the sectors each item concerns, following Indonesia's Perpres 82/2022. → Analysis
    • Geography: target, source and hosting countries, Indonesian provinces, and an International / National (Indonesia) view across the whole interface. → Geography and Indonesia

    Everything goes through one pipeline into one connected set of entities, so a scam report links to the malware it installs, and a ransomware campaign to the CVEs it exploits. Two dashboards show the results, one for cyber threats and one for scams and fraud, and the data can be shared through the REST API, STIX / TAXII, IOC feeds, webhooks and MISP.

    Where to start¶

    • Installing JATMP? Start with Getting started, then Configuration.
    • Using it day to day? Read Using JATMP for a tour of the interface.
    • Want to know where a score or verdict comes from? Go to How JATMP works, which follows one article from the feed to the dashboard.
    • Changing the code? Start with Development.
